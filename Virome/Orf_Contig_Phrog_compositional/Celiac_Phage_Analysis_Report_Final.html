Previous effect size plots were misleading! They only showed genes with valid p-values (3-15% of data), hiding massive effect sizes (-12,886 to +6,198 log odds ratios) that represent perfect biomarkers with sparse data issues.

Figure 6 (UPDATED): Complete PA effect size analysis. Red points show large effects without p-values (perfect separation), blue points show statistically testable effects. Range: -906 to +354 log odds ratios.
Figure 7 (UPDATED): Complete abundance effect size analysis showing all 763 results. 77 genes (10.1%) have valid p-values, 5 are significant. Range: -476 to +298 log fold changes.

US Cohort - Extended Analysis

Figure 8 (NEW): US cohort PA effects with extreme range (-12,886 to +6,198). Only 56/1050 results (5.3%) have valid p-values, but 6 are significant. Shows potential perfect biomarkers.

📊 Effect Size Insights

  • Hidden Large Effects: Original plots missed effects >1000x larger due to sparse data
  • Perfect Biomarkers: Extreme effect sizes represent genes completely absent/present in one group
